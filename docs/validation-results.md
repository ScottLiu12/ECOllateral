## Synthetic regression benchmark

Each cooling technology has 1,600 generated records spanning six years. Both candidates
are fitted on the first 60% of distinct dates and selected using calibration RMSE in the
next 20%. This table uses the final 20% of dates and physically constrained forecasts.
Synthetic targets are generated by `src/demo.py`; they are not measured water use.

| Cooling technology | Selected model | Held-out R² | MAE, MGD | RMSE, MGD | Nominal 90% interval coverage |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Cooling tower | XGBoost | 0.99808 | 0.003668 | 0.004583 | 88.29% |
| Direct evaporative | XGBoost | 0.99728 | 0.003191 | 0.004048 | 87.99% |
| Dry air cooled | RandomForest | Undefined | 0.000000 | 0.000000 | 100.00% |

The evaporative models exceed the requested R² > 0.80 and MAE < 0.05 MGD targets on
this synthetic response surface. Their interval coverage is **below** the nominal 90%
on the finite held-out sample. Reported R² must not be interpreted as a confidence
probability, and nominal coverage must not be claimed as verified 90% field coverage.
Dry cooling has a constant zero target in the stated scope, so R² is undefined.

Full raw/bounded candidate metrics, split dates, row counts, and calibration radii are
in `data/processed/demo/benchmark.json` and reproduced by `python -m src.cli demo`.

## Synthetic drought missingness

The executed notebook used 365 station dates, 121 drought-window dates, and 50 seeded
replicates per rate. Both methods received the same masks within each replicate.

| Removed drought records | Records removed per replicate | Spatial prediction variance, MGD² | Linear prediction variance, MGD² |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 10% | 12 | 3.295e-9 | 6.001e-9 |
| 25% | 30 | 6.314e-9 | 2.212e-8 |
| 40% | 48 | 9.054e-9 | 4.092e-8 |

All six combinations had 100% of drought forecast perturbations within ±0.05 MGD and
every empirical central 90% band within that tolerance. This result is expected for
strongly correlated synthetic neighbors and a model with modest streamflow sensitivity.
It does not validate gap filling under real droughts. The tests also use deliberately
biased donors and confirm that the analysis flags instability instead of always passing.

The separate toy groundwater reconstruction retains meters. Spatial MAE ranges from
0.0113 to 0.0127 m; temporal MAE ranges from 0.000160 to 0.000385 m. No groundwater
depth is passed off as an MGD regression feature.

Notebook outputs are in `data/processed/research/`: `missingness_summary.csv`,
`missingness_bands.csv`, `groundwater_reconstruction.csv`, two PNG figures, and
`missingness.executed.ipynb`. Both figures were inspected for readable units, legends,
drought labeling, and tolerance bounds. The tracked source notebook remains unexecuted
so generated images and machine-specific paths do not bloat Git history.
